# Design rules

Learned over many rounds of real post graphics. The person's brand kit wins where it says something different.

## Defaults (when the kit does not say)
- Size: portrait 1080 × 1350. Square 1080 × 1080 on request.
- Margins: 90 px on every side.
- Text: at least 26 px. Headline at least 80 px. Body text 30 to 40 px.
- Fonts: Poppins (headings, 700 to 800) and Inter (body, 400 to 600).
- Background pattern: grid, 44 px cells, 1 px lines in the primary at about 9% opacity, softly faded with a mask.

## Background
- Never a plain flat background. Always the kit's soft pattern on the background colour.
- A few outlined corner shapes (squares tilted about 32 degrees, 3 px border in a light tint), partly off the top-left and bottom-right edges. Outlines only, no filled blocks.
- A thin frame: a 2 px outline in the accent colour, about 26 to 50 px out from the main card or strip.
- Light style background: a very light tint of the primary (never pure white, never the full brand colour, never black). Dark style is the exception and only when the kit allows it.

## Layout
- One idea per block. At most 8 blocks, most graphics need 3 to 5. Every block has real content.
- Give every block room. If it feels crowded, cut words before you shrink anything.
- One clear focal point: the number, the photo, or the headline. The eye should know where to land.
- Headline key words in the primary colour. Use the accent for highlights and lines. No other colours.
- Number pills (01, 02, 03) for steps and lists.
- Profile bar on most graphics:
  - Small: round photo and name. For most explainers.
  - Large: photo, name, what they do, and a follow-style button. For posts that should feel personal.
- Recognisable frames work well when they fit the content: a LinkedIn post frame, a phone screen, a code editor with a folder tree, a chat bubble, a before/after split, a timeline.

## Cards, shadows and photos
- Cards: the card colour, a 1 px border in a light tint, rounded corners (18 to 28 px).
- Shadows stay subtle: small blur, low opacity (for example `0 6px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.08)`). Never heavy, dark shadows.
- Photos of the person come only from files they share. Crop, resize and position only. Never edit their face, clothes or background, and never generate a photo of a real person.
- Don't over-crop faces: show the head and shoulders, not just the face. No blur on the person.
- Screenshots go in a clean card. If a screenshot is low-contrast, place it on a card that makes it readable instead of changing the screenshot.
- Other people's faces, logos or brands: only with the person's OK that they have the right (for example a commenter on their own post). Blur names on screenshots if they ask.

## Text
- All words come from the person's own post or notes. Never invent text, numbers, quotes, names or results.
- Short lines. A graphic is read in 2 seconds: headline, then the focal point, then the details.
- No tagline strips like "good content builds trust" unless the person wrote them.

## Not generic AI
Avoid anything that makes it look generated:
- Random purple, violet or blue-to-purple gradients.
- Glowing orbs, neon light streaks, stock 3D shapes, abstract blobs.
- Emojis as decoration (unless the kit says they use them).
- Every element the same size and weight, with nothing standing out.
- Decorative photos that do not relate to the content.
- Text-only cards with nothing to stop the scroll. If it is only text, give it a frame, a big number, or a photo.

## Before you show it
- Nothing cut off or overlapping. Nothing under 26 px. Contrast readable everywhere.
- Margins respected. The pattern, corner shapes and frame are there.
- Look at the PNG yourself, then fix it, then show it.
